Features

Everything you just watched happens on your own page, with your own content.

Cursors

A whole team on your page

Sixteen named, colored cursors (ten on mobile) fly to each element, draw a selection box with its real size, and leave a trail behind them. Elements on screen are built first.

16 cursorsSelection boxesTrails
Text

Typed letter by letter

Headings and paragraphs are typed live, with a caret in the cursor's color. The layout never jumps while it types.

No layout shift
Scroll

Follows the build

The page scrolls down on its own as the build moves on, and stops as soon as the visitor scrolls.

Auto-scroll
Controls

A control bar

Toggle auto-scroll, change the speed, build everything at once, or replay the whole show.

1× · 2× · 4×Replay
Accessibility

Respects reduced motion

If the system asks for less motion, the page shows up finished, with a button to start the animation on demand.

prefers-reduced-motion
Safety net

Never a blank page

If the script can't load or start, the page simply displays normally after a few seconds.

Fallback

Get started in 3 steps

Step 1

Copy two files

Download css/chantier.css and js/chantier.js from the repository and put them in your project.

Step 2

Add them to your head

Load the script in the head, without defer, so it can hide the blocks before the page appears.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="css/chantier.css">
<script src="js/chantier.js"></script>
Step 3

Mark your elements

Add the class b to anything a cursor should build, and b t to text that should be typed.

<section data-frame>
  <h1 class="b t">My page</h1>
  <img class="b" src="photo.jpg" alt="">
</section>

Options

data-logo

Text of the central logo during the scan. Defaults to the first letters of the page title.

data-agents

Number of cursors. 16 by default, 10 on small screens.

data-noms

Your own cursor names, separated by commas.

data-lang

Language of the control bar: en or fr. Defaults to the language of your page.

data-auto

Set it to false to start the build yourself with Chantier.lancer().
